Owner controls

Hidden owner panel. In this offline HTML prototype, these controls are local-only and are not secure authentication or a real approval server.

For a public Play Store app, move owner authentication, approvals, and access checks to a secure server. A hidden tap sequence is only a convenience, not security.

01 · Find your phone

Searches the built-in starter catalogue. If your model is missing, choose a brand and enter the model manually; the app will label it as unlisted rather than claim it does not exist.

02 · Songs and audio

Play a song while you practise, or choose an audio file already saved on your phone. This is a practice player; it does not add music to a Free Fire recording or an exported video.

Use audio you own or have permission to play. Some sites block direct playback; use the file picker if a URL does not work.

03 · Your device

Enter your exact model. Unknown models can still get an estimated profile.

Phone details are used to choose a starting point; this page does not secretly read hardware or Free Fire data.

04 · Current settings (optional)

Enter your current values if you know them. Otherwise, generate a starting profile.

05 · Generate a profile

Recommendations use a transparent baseline and your selected device/play style. They are not claimed to be scientifically verified for your phone until you test them.

What this version can and cannot do

Works offline in a modern browser after the file is saved. It recommends and tracks settings; it does not change Free Fire settings automatically, inspect game memory, or make bullets lock onto heads. Use the values in the game's settings and test them fairly.
